## 5. The Product Audit — 10 findings, 4 themes

(Product-opportunity audit, NOT a CRO audit. WSO is profitable and established. The audit shows Nihal already thinking like the PM, finding where the product surface has untapped opportunity.)

### Theme A: The two-stack seam

**Finding 01 — WSO is two products on two stacks, and they don't share a spine.**
- The main site (wallstreetoasis.com) and WSO Academy run on visibly different systems (confirmed by the captured screenshots: different visual language, different nav, different feel).
- A 19-year-old Drupal community monolith + a modern Academy platform.
- The PM hired here is the connective tissue. The free-member-to-Academy-applicant signal isn't instrumented across the seam.
- Severity: Strategic · ongoing

**Finding 02 — Shared identity is missing across the two stacks.**
- A logged-in community member and an Academy student are likely two separate identity records.
- Means: WSO can't see "this forum power-user is a high-intent Academy lead" without manual stitching.
- Severity: High

**Finding 03 — The Drupal monolith's age is a roadmap constraint nobody has scoped.**
- 19-year-old codebase. Every new feature on the community side either fights the Drupal version or gets built as a bolt-on.
- The PM should own an honest "what can Drupal still do, what needs to move off it" assessment as a week-one deliverable.
- Severity: Strategic

### Theme B: The archive is a moat used like a band-aid

**Finding 04 — 17 years of forum Q&A, and the AI bot posts in threads instead of powering a discovery layer.**
- The AI bot trained on WSO content currently shows up as a participant in forum threads.
- That's a band-aid. The actual product is "ask the WSO archive": a RAG layer over 17 years of Q&A that answers a finance-recruiting question instantly with cited sources.
- Severity: High · the single biggest unbuilt product

**Finding 05 — The 100,000+ interview/salary datapoints are a stat on the homepage, not a product.**
- Home page says "100,000+ interview & salary datapoints across thousands of firms."
- It's surfaced as a credibility number. It should be a queryable, sliceable product surface (compensation explorer, firm-by-firm interview-difficulty index).
- Severity: High

**Finding 06 — The forum's search is pre-AI-era in an AI-era category.**
- Users now expect to ask a question and get a synthesized answer. WSO's forum likely still does keyword search over threads.
- Competitors will ship "ask AI" over their (smaller) content. WSO has the bigger corpus and should win this, but only if the PM builds it.
- Severity: High

### Theme C: The community-to-Academy funnel

**Finding 07 — The funnel exists as steps, not as an instrumented product.**
- Free community member → Academy waitlist → application → admissions call. Each step is real.
- But it's not instrumented as a measured funnel with conversion rates per step and experiment slots.
- Severity: High

**Finding 08 — The highest-intent Academy leads are invisible because of the identity seam (Finding 02).**
- A forum member reading 30 IB-recruiting threads is the warmest possible Academy lead. WSO can't currently see that signal because community-side behavior doesn't flow to the Academy CRM (Keap).
- Severity: High

### Theme D: Ops latency

**Finding 09 — Service latency is a Trustpilot-visible problem.**
- A Trustpilot reviewer reports resume review took "more than one month" with "slow communication."
- The JD explicitly asks for internal-ops automation. Resume-review SLA is a concrete, measurable automation target.
- Severity: High · directly maps to the JD

**Finding 10 — Mentor-matching and admissions triage are manual-heavy.**
- 3,500+ mentors. Matching students to mentors and triaging admissions applications is human-heavy work.
- An AI-assisted matching + triage layer is the on-brand automation the JD wants.
- Severity: Medium-High

## 6. The 4 BESPOKE workflows (mapped to the JD's two halves)

### Workflow 01: Spec-to-Sprint Pipeline
- **Maps to JD:** "dev team coordination and roadmap (Ukraine-based engineers): specs, sprint cadence, prioritization, shipping"
- **What it is:** how the India PM turns an ambiguous founder idea into an engineer-ready spec, runs sprint cadence async across a 3-timezone team (India PM, Ukraine engineers, US team).
- **Flow:** idea capture → 1-page spec (problem, scope, acceptance criteria, out-of-scope) → async review → sprint slot → daily async standup digest → ship → retro note
- **Anchor metric:** spec-to-ship cycle time; % of sprints that ship on cadence
- **Stack:** Linear or GitHub Projects + Loom for async walkthroughs + a written-spec template

### Workflow 02: AI Interview Coach
- **Maps to JD:** "AI-powered initiatives across our mentorship platform" + the existing "AI mock interview platform, 8 industry tracks"
- **What it is:** the production engineering of WSO's mock-interview AI, built on the 10,000+ mock-interview-score database. Scores a candidate's answer across sub-categories, benchmarks against the database, returns specific drills.
- **Flow:** candidate records answer → Whisper transcript → Claude scores vs rubric + benchmarks against the 10,000-score corpus → specific feedback + next drill
- **Anchor metric:** score-improvement curve per candidate over a cohort; correlation of AI-coach usage with placement outcomes
- **Why it wins:** the moat (10,000+ scores) becomes the product

### Workflow 03: Forum Archive Intelligence
- **Maps to JD:** AI/LLM product work + solves audit Findings 04, 05, 06
- **What it is:** RAG over 17 years of WSO forum Q&A + the 100,000 interview/salary datapoints. "Ask the archive." A finance-recruiting question gets a synthesized answer with cited source threads.
- **Flow:** user question → embeddings retrieval over the indexed archive → Claude synthesis with citations → answer + linked source threads + "still want more? join the Academy"
- **Anchor metric:** archive-query volume; query-to-Academy-waitlist conversion
- **Why it wins:** turns a dead Drupal archive into a live, queryable product AND a funnel into Academy

### Workflow 04: Ops Automation Engine
- **Maps to JD:** "internal ops automation" + solves audit Findings 09, 10
- **What it is:** automating the manual ops work. Resume-review intake + SLA tracking, mentor-matching, admissions-application triage.
- **Flow:** resume submitted → auto-acknowledge + SLA clock starts → Claude pre-pass flags the obvious fixes → human reviewer gets a pre-annotated doc → SLA dashboard catches anything aging past target
- **Anchor metric:** resume-review turnaround (target: days, not "more than a month"); mentor-match time
- **Stack:** n8n + Claude + Keap integration
