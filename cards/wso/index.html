What I'd build

Four workflows, one per quadrant of the role.

System 01 dev coordination

Spec-to-Sprint Pipeline

An ambiguous founder idea becomes an engineer-ready spec the Ukraine team picks up without a meeting. Async across three timezones. This half of the role is what I did at Gamezop.
System 02 AI initiative

AI Interview Coach

WSO already scores mock interviews against 10,000+ scores. This turns that corpus into a coaching loop: a candidate gets a score, then the one specific drill that fixes their weakest sub-score.
System 03 new product

Forum Archive Intelligence

17 years of forum Q&A. "Ask the archive": a question gets a synthesized answer with cited source threads. The archive becomes a queryable product, and a quiet path into Academy.
System 04 internal ops

Ops Automation Engine

The JD asks for internal-ops automation. Resume-review SLA tracking, an AI pre-pass for reviewers, mentor-matching, admissions triage. The manual work, made fast.
