System 04 · Ops Automation Engine · internal ops

A resume review took "more than a month."
That's a measurable target.

The JD asks for internal ops automation by name. A public Trustpilot review hands us the first target: resume review running over a month, with slow communication. This system fixes the kind of work that scales linearly with headcount. Resume-review SLA tracking, an AI pre-pass that annotates the obvious fixes before a human opens the doc, mentor-matching, admissions triage. Not replacing the human judgment. Removing the sorting and the waiting.

How it runs

STACK
n8n orchestrates. Claude does the pre-pass annotation and the matching reasoning. Keap (the existing CRM) holds the records. An SLA dashboard catches anything aging past target.
REPLACES
The resume that sits in an inbox for a month. The mentor-match done from memory. The admissions pile sorted by hand. Work that grows with headcount instead of with software.
ANCHOR METRIC
Resume-review turnaround, target measured in days not "more than a month." Mentor-match time. Percentage of admissions applications a human spends real time on (the genuine maybes, not the obvious sorts).

Sample · what the human reviewer opens (pre-annotated)
Claude pre-pass found 4 mechanical fixes so the human starts on substance:
Bullet 3 leads with a duty, not a result. Flag for "lead with the number."
Two bullets exceed two lines. Banking resumes are one line per bullet.
Inconsistent date format between two roles.
No deal or transaction value quantified anywhere. Big one for IB.
The human reviewer now spends their time on the judgment call (is this story compelling for PE vs IB), not on catching formatting. That's where the month becomes days.
