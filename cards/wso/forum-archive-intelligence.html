System 03 · Forum Archive Intelligence · new product

17 years of answers, buried.
This is the shovel.

Since 2006, finance candidates have been asking and answering recruiting questions on the WSO forum. That archive is the deepest moat in the category. Right now an AI bot posts inside threads, which helps a little. The real product is "ask the WSO archive": a user types a question, gets a synthesized answer with cited source threads, in seconds. The dead archive becomes a live, queryable product. And every answer ends with a quiet path into the Academy.

How it runs

STACK
The 17-year forum corpus chunked and embedded into a vector store. A retrieval pass over the index. Claude synthesizes the answer with inline citations. Renders as a box above forum search results.
REPLACES
Keyword search that returns 40 thread links. The AI bot that posts inside threads as a band-aid. The user who wanted an answer and got a reading list.

Archive-query volume. The conversion one: archive-query to Academy-waitlist rate. The archive becomes a measurable funnel into the paid product.

Sample · "ask the archive" answer box
Cited, not invented
QUESTION
"How early should I start networking for IB summer analyst recruiting?"
ANSWER FROM THE ARCHIVE
The consistent answer across 17 years of WSO threads: start the summer before your target recruiting cycle, roughly 12 to 18 months out. For a target sophomore-summer internship, that means networking in freshman spring. The threads agree the early mover advantage is real and front-loaded. Cold outreach in the off-cycle gets responses; the same outreach during peak recruiting competes with everyone else's.
