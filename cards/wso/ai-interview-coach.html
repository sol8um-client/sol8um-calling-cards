System 02 · AI Interview Coach · the AI initiative

WSO has 10,000 mock-interview scores.
That's the product, not the stat.

The JD mentions an AI mock-interview platform across 8 industry tracks. WSO already scores mock interviews against a database of over 10,000 scores. Most companies would kill for that corpus. The opportunity is to make it the engine of a real coaching loop: a candidate records an answer, gets scored against the 10,000, and most importantly gets the one specific drill that moves their weakest sub-score. Not a number. A next action.

How it runs

STACK
Whisper for the transcript. Claude scores the answer against a rubric and benchmarks it against the 10,000-score corpus. A drill library mapped to each weak sub-category. The Academy dashboard renders it.
REPLACES
A mock-interview score that tells a candidate "72/100" and nothing about what to do next. The gap between knowing you're weak and knowing how to fix it.
ANCHOR METRIC
Score-improvement curve per candidate across a cohort. The real one: correlation between AI-coach usage and the Academy's job-placement outcome, the number the "Top Job Offer Guaranteed" promise rides on.
