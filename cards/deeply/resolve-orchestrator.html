How it runs

STACK
Claude (translator + commitment-extractor) + Next.js (real-time UI) + Postgres (conversation state) + LangChain (prompt orchestration) + Supabase (auth + state sync between partners). Built with Claude Code.
REPLACES
"AI summarizes your fight" magic-box pattern. Most relationship AI products are black boxes; this one shows the work between input and output.

Sample · Phase 1 to Phase 2, the translation in detail
Transparent, not magic
RAW (PARTNER A WROTE)
"You never put your phone down at dinner. I feel like I'm dating your inbox. It's been like this for months and you don't even notice."
RECEIVABLE (CLAUDE TRANSLATED)
"Connection at dinner matters to me, and I want us both to be present. Could we both keep phones away during dinner? I've felt distant for a while and want us to fix this together."
WHAT THE TRANSLATION DID
Kept the request. Dropped "you never," "I feel like I'm dating your inbox" (memorable but accusatory), "you don't even notice" (story about partner's awareness). Reframed as a shared problem with a specific proposal. Both partners see both versions. The AI never hides the work.
