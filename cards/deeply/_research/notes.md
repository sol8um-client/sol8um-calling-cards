### Block 02: Resolve flow demo
- A 60-second video / animated mockup showing the bilateral flow in action
- Currently described in JD prose; needs to be visible
- Pattern: split-screen showing partner A's raw input, then AI-translated version, then partner B's receivable read
- Trust matters here: viewer needs to see the AI's role transparently, not magical

### Block 03: Trust signals for mental-health-adjacent product
- Therapist endorsements (even 1-2 named therapists adds credibility)
- "4.56/5 validation" methodology disclosed (sample size, panel composition)
- Privacy commitment ("your conversations never leave the encrypted store")

- Crisis-handoff statement: "If you're in crisis, here's where to go" (links to 988 / national hotlines)

### Block 04: Bilateral CTA mechanic

- Solution: primary CTA is "Send invite to your partner," not "Sign up"

- Partner-2 magic-link lands at the specific conversation context, not a generic signup page

### Block 05: Founder note — Felix's voice

## 6. The 4 BESPOKE workflows (mapped 1:1 to JD's "What you'll do" lines)

### Workflow 01: Bilateral Onboarding Engine
- **Solves:** the two-partner problem at the product layer (and at the marketing layer)
- **Flow:** partner A signs up → enters context (the conflict in 1 sentence) → magic link generated → partner B receives via SMS/email → partner B opens link, lands at the SPECIFIC conversation seed → both onboarded simultaneously
- **JD line mapped:** "building bilateral workflows"
- **Anchor metric:** % of partner-A signups whose partner-B activates within 48 hours. Industry baseline for couples apps: 25-35%. Target 60%+.

### Workflow 02: Resolve Conversation Orchestrator
- **Solves:** the core product flow itself
- **Flow:** raw emotional input from partner A → Claude translates to receivable language → partner B reads + responds → AI scaffolds the negotiation toward specific commitments → both sign
- **Stack:** Claude as translator + safety layer, structured conversation states in Postgres, real-time UI in Next.js, LangChain for prompt orchestration
- **JD line mapped:** "orchestrating LLM services, building conversation flows"
- **Anchor metric:** completion rate (% of started Resolves that reach signed commitment). 4.56/5 validation suggests this is already strong; the workflow is the production-engineering of the validated flow.

### Workflow 03: Outcome Tracking + Habit Loop
- **Solves:** "did the resolution stick?" — the question every relationship app dodges
- **Flow:** at signing, commitments get extracted as structured data (action, owner, date). 7/14/30-day check-ins ping each partner: "Did [partner A] do [commitment X] by [date Y]?" Both partners answer. System surfaces patterns: which commitments stick, which break, which conversations need a follow-up Resolve.
- **JD line mapped:** "Bridge the gap between product vision and working software... Run experiments, gather feedback, iterate fast"
- **Anchor metric:** 30-day commitment-honored rate. The single number that proves Deeply works.

### Workflow 04: Trust + Safety Ops
- **Solves:** the operational layer a mental-health-adjacent product needs from day one
- **Flow:** Claude flags conversations suggesting harm (self-harm, abuse, crisis) → instant escalation to a human triage queue → partner directed to immediate-help resources (988, local hotlines) → conversation paused with care messaging → optional therapist-referral integration for handoff
- **JD line mapped (implicit):** behavioral science / mental health adjacency
- **Anchor metric:** crisis-detection precision and recall on a curated test set. Conversation pause + handoff time. The number that lets Deeply sleep at night.

These four are NOT Cielo's, SciSpace's, Ventracle's, Social Capital's, REWORK's, Rank Math's, WOWCube's, Marvin's. Reasoned from THIS product and THIS hybrid PM+Engineer role.
