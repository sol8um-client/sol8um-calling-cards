Did the resolution stick?
That's the only question that matters.

Every relationship app I've looked at dodges this question. They ship the conversation, then the user is on their own. The 4.56/5 validation score tells us the Resolve moment scores well. It doesn't yet tell us whether the signed commitments survive past day 3. This system answers that. At signing, commitments get extracted as structured data: action, owner, deadline, check-in cadence. 7/14/30-day pings ask each partner: did it happen? Patterns surface. The single number that proves Deeply works.

How it runs

STACK
Claude extracts commitments as structured data at signing. Postgres stores them. n8n fires 7/14/30-day check-in jobs. Twilio + Resend deliver. Each partner answers independently; system reconciles.
REPLACES
"Hope your resolution lasted." The closed loop on whether Deeply actually works.
ANCHOR METRIC
30-day commitment-honored rate. The single number that proves the product. Becomes the headline stat in your next pitch deck and the foundation for the Credit Karma analogy (a "relationship health score" needs underlying data).
