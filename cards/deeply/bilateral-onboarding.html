System 01 · Bilateral Onboarding Engine · Week 1-2

The partner-2 problem,
solved at the product layer.

Every relationship app fails on the same thing: partner A signs up enthusiastically, partner B never gets pulled in. This system solves it as a structural product decision, not a marketing-trick. Partner A signs up, names the conflict in one sentence, generates a magic link with that context baked in. Partner B opens the link, lands at the specific conversation seed (not a generic signup), reads partner A's framing, and joins the Resolve. Both onboarded in one bilateral motion.

How it runs

STACK
Next.js + Supabase Auth (magic-link generation) + Twilio (SMS delivery) + Resend (email) + Postgres for conversation-seed state. Built with Claude Code.
REPLACES
The standard "sign up, then invite your partner separately" flow that loses 60-70% of partner-2s. Replaces it with a single bilateral mechanic.
ANCHOR METRIC
% of partner-A signups whose partner-B activates within 48 hours. Industry baseline for couples apps: 25-35%. Target 60%+.
