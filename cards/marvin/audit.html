A
The PLG-vs-sales-led identity confusion
Three findings. The product motion is PLG. The marketing motion is sales-led. The Growth PM closes that gap.
01

"Get demo" dominates the homepage CTA hierarchy despite a working free tier existing.

Critical · 30 min A/B
EVIDENCE
Hero CTA: "Get demo" (large, primary, accent color). "Sign up" appears top-right, small. For a PLG product with a real $0/year Free tier (5 files/month, 5 AI moderated interviews, no card required), this CTA hierarchy is inverted. Self-serve signups are the volume play. Demos are the high-touch enterprise play.
heymarvin.com HERO · CTA HIERARCHY INVERTED
"GET DEMO" PRIMARY · "SIGN UP" SECONDARY (TOP RIGHT, SMALL)
SECONDARY HERO CTA: "GET DEMO" AGAIN
QUICK WIN
A/B test the CTA hierarchy. Variant A: "Sign up free" primary, "Get demo" secondary. Variant B: split CTA card showing both paths with framing ("Solo researcher? Sign up free. Team of 5+? Get a demo."). 30-minute test config. The PM hired into this role would likely flip this within their first month.
Estimated lift on self-serve signups +significant, persistent
02

5-user minimum on Standard tier creates a solo-to-team activation chasm. The 1-to-5 ramp is missing.

Critical · Strategic · 2-4 weeks
EVIDENCE
Free: solo only, $0, 5 files/month. Standard: custom pricing, 5 users minimum, billed annually. Enterprise: custom pricing, 5 users minimum. A solo Free user who's hooked has nowhere to go. They can't upgrade to a 1-user paid tier. They can't always rally 4 colleagues overnight. The chasm sits at the seam between activation and monetization.
heymarvin.com/pricing PRICING · 1-TO-5 RAMP MISSING
FREE (SOLO ONLY) → STANDARD (5 USER MIN). NO 1-TO-4 USER OPTION.
QUICK WIN
Two paths. (a) Add a Solo Pro tier at $X/mo for individual researchers who want more than Free but can't bring 5 teammates yet. Captures the in-between buyer. (b) Build a team-formation flow (System 02 in the workflows section): Free user invites teammate-by-teammate, each invite expands the seat-pool free, when 5 teammates join the team auto-converts to Standard trial. The product engineers the team formation.
03

Free tier is unlimited-time with no Free-to-Paid urgency engineered.

High · 1-2 weeks
EVIDENCE
Free: $0 per year forever. 5 files/month. No trial clock, no usage-based nudge that triggers paid features unlock, no progressive disclosure of paid value. Industry benchmark: PLG products that compound (Notion's block limit, Loom's video count, Linear's seat count) engineer Free-to-Paid pressure into the product itself. Marvin's Free tier is generous but creates no upgrade pressure.
heymarvin.com/pricing FREE TIER · NO URGENCY ENGINEERED
FREE = UNLIMITED TIME, NO TRIAL CLOCK, NO PRESSURE
QUICK WIN
Engineer one specific paid-feature pressure point. Examples: "Your 5 monthly files reset on day X. Upgrade to keep your insights queryable." Or: "Agentic Ask AI on premium queries (limit 3/month free)." The point isn't to break Free; it's to make the upgrade decision concrete and tied to real value the user already loves.
B
Onboarding and activation friction
Three findings. The activation moment is unpicked. The discovery path is broken.
04

heymarvin.com/product returns 404. A curious visitor has no self-serve discovery path.

High · 1 day
EVIDENCE
heymarvin.com/product is a 404. The natural URL a curious visitor types when they want to learn more is broken. Result: visitors either Get-Demo or leave. There's no in-between for the self-serve user who wants to understand the product before signing up.
CURRENT · 404
GET https://heymarvin.com/product
> HTTP/2 404 Not Found
A standard URL a curious visitor would try. Broken.
SUGGESTED · /product LANDING
Self-serve product tour. 4 sections matching the 4 hero capabilities (AI Moderated Interview, Agentic Ask AI, repository search, integrations). Each with a 30-second video, a representative screenshot, and a "Try it free" CTA back to signup. Ships in a day with the existing product collateral.
05

Activation moment is unclear. The site never picks a hero job-to-be-done.

Critical · Strategic · 1-2 weeks
EVIDENCE
The homepage carries multiple value props simultaneously: AI Moderated Interviews, Agentic Ask AI, repository search, AI insights, 30+ integrations. Different visitor segments interpret different value props as the lead. The site speaks to all of them, which is the same as speaking to none of them clearly. For a Growth PM, picking the activation moment is the highest-leverage decision.
heymarvin.com HOMEPAGE · FIVE VALUE PROPS, NO HERO ONE
HERO TRIES TO COVER MULTIPLE JOBS-TO-BE-DONE AT ONCE
QUICK WIN
Pull activation data from Mixpanel/Amplitude. Find the action that correlates highest with day-7 retention and day-30 paid conversion. THAT is the activation moment. Architect onboarding around it. The other features are post-activation upgrade hooks, not first-impression sells. (System 01 owns this directly.)
06

Onboarding is human-assisted at scale. Multiple G2 reviews mention "onboarding calls."

Strategic · ongoing 4-8 weeks
EVIDENCE
G2 reviews repeatedly cite "the onboarding call provided by the team was insightful." Implies high-touch onboarding for paid users. High-touch onboarding doesn't scale to PLG. Either it ramps down (and self-serve onboarding becomes the new normal) or PLG remains a marketing veneer over a sales-led motion.
CURRENT · HUMAN-ASSISTED
Customer signs up → books an onboarding call → CSM walks them through the product → customer activates.

Doesn't scale past ~50 onboardings/week. Caps growth at the CSM team's bandwidth.
SUGGESTED · IN-PRODUCT TOUR
Customer signs up → in-product guided tour with sample data preloaded → AI-assisted "import your first interview" walkthrough → customer activates without human touch.

Scales infinitely. CSM team becomes the escalation path, not the default path.

Conversion architecture
Two findings. Comparison content is in the wrong place. The integrations gap is publicly visible.

The /resources/dovetail-alternatives page exists and is well-written. Placement signals "we want SEO traffic on this query," not "we want the buyer who's in active comparison mode to convert." A buyer comparing Marvin vs Dovetail right now has to know to navigate to Resources to find this. Most don't.

08

Integrations gap publicly: Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet, Otter all absent from the public list. Surprising for a research-call platform.

High · 1 day
EVIDENCE
Public integrations page lists FigJam, Miro, Confluence, Notion, Slack, Asana, Jira, Rally, Salesforce, Pendo, Qualtrics, Google Forms, UserTesting. Zoom, Teams, Meet, Otter, HubSpot, Zapier are absent. For a research-call analysis platform, customer interviews happen on Zoom/Teams/Meet. Either these integrations exist and need to be surfaced, or they're missing. Both outcomes fail the buyer's "can it import my interviews?" test.
CURRENT · PUBLIC INTEGRATIONS LIST
FigJam · Miro · Confluence · Notion
Slack · Asana · Jira · Rally
Salesforce · Pendo · Qualtrics
Google Forms · UserTesting

Missing from public list:
Zoom · Teams · Meet · Otter · HubSpot · Zapier
SUGGESTED · FIX
If they exist: add to the public integrations page immediately. These are the ones a buyer specifically searches for ("does Marvin work with Zoom?").

If they don't exist: roadmap them. Zoom and Teams are table stakes for this category. Customer interview audio capture starts there.

Either way, the public listing should answer the "can it import my interviews?" question without friction.
D
Retention and expansion
Two findings. The viral loop is missing. The traction metric is buried.
09

No public referral or team-invite mechanic for a fundamentally collaborative product.

High · 2-4 weeks
EVIDENCE
A researcher invites a teammate to see an insight. That's the natural viral loop for a collaborative research tool. Marvin's marketing surface doesn't have the magic-link / "invite-a-teammate" entry point as a growth lever. Inside the app it likely exists; on the marketing site it's invisible.
CURRENT · NO PUBLIC INVITE LOOP
No "invite a teammate" CTA on the marketing site. No magic-link landing page for invitees. No referral-incentive mention.

A researcher who finds an insight worth sharing has no clean path to bring a colleague in via the public site.
SUGGESTED · INSIGHT-LEVEL INVITE LOOP
In-product: "Share this insight with a teammate" generates a magic link. Invitee clicks, lands at the specific insight (gated by 1-click signup), sees the value before signing up.

On the marketing site: a "How sharing works" sub-page within /product. Plus visible referral incentive ("Bring a teammate, both get an extra 5 files/month").
10

Headline traction metric ("350% revenue growth, 13K users, 10 Fortune 100") is buried in case studies instead of being the above-the-fold social proof.

Medium · 30 min
EVIDENCE
For a self-serve signup CTA, this stat ("350% revenue growth, 13K users, 10 Fortune 100s") is the strongest single conversion driver and it's invisible. Above-the-fold currently has hero copy + logo strip. Adding the metric strip as a third row would make the social proof concrete and quantitative, not just logo-decorative.
heymarvin.com ABOVE FOLD · METRIC STRIP MISSING
METRIC STRIP "350% GROWTH · 13K USERS · 10 FORTUNE 100" SHOULD LIVE HERE
QUICK WIN
Add a 3-stat strip directly below the hero copy: 350% revenue growth this year · 13,000+ researchers · 10 Fortune 100 customers. 30-minute add. Concrete, quantitative, scannable. Lifts signup-CTA click-through measurably (industry pattern: 10-25% lift on hero-stat additions).
