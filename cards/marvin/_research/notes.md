## 4. The 10 audit findings (4-theme spine bespoke to a PLG-product wearing sales-led marketing clothes)

### Theme A: The PLG-vs-sales-led identity confusion (THE loudest gap; this IS the role)

**Finding 01 — "Get demo" dominates the homepage CTA hierarchy despite a working free tier existing.**
- Hero CTA: "Get demo" (primary, large, accent color). "Sign up" (secondary, small, top-right).
- For a PLG product with a real free tier, this CTA hierarchy is inverted. Self-serve signups are the volume play; demos are the high-touch enterprise play.
- The PM hired into this role would likely flip this within their first month and A/B test it on Day 1.
- Severity: Critical · 30 min A/B test · day-1 priority
- Shot: marvin-home.png with hero CTA highlighted

**Finding 02 — 5-user minimum on Standard tier creates a solo-to-team activation chasm.**
- Free tier: solo only. Smallest paid tier: 5-user minimum.
- A solo Free user who's hooked has nowhere to go. They can't upgrade to a 1-user Standard, and they can't necessarily convince 4 colleagues to join overnight.
- The 1→5 ramp is missing. Free converts on accident, Standard converts on enterprise sales motion.
- Severity: Critical · Strategic · 2-4 weeks (pricing redesign)
- Shot: marvin-pricing.png with the 3-tier structure highlighted

**Finding 03 — Free tier is unlimited-time with no Free→Paid urgency engineered.**
- Free has no trial clock, no usage-based nudge that triggers paid features unlock, no progressive disclosure of paid value.
- Industry benchmark: most successful PLG products engineer the Free→Paid pressure (e.g., Notion's block limit, Loom's video count, Linear's seat count). Marvin's Free tier is generous but creates no upgrade pressure.
- Severity: High · 1-2 weeks experimentation
- Shot: marvin-pricing.png with Free tier highlighted

### Theme B: Onboarding and activation friction

**Finding 04 — No /product page (404). A curious visitor has no self-serve discovery path.**
- heymarvin.com/product returns 404. The natural URL a visitor types when they want to learn more — broken.
- Result: visitors either Get-Demo or leave. There's no in-between for the self-serve user.
- Severity: High · 1 day to ship a basic /product page
- Shot: compare-cell showing 404 vs proposed /product page outline

**Finding 05 — Activation moment is unclear. The site never picks a hero job-to-be-done.**
- Product variants: AI Moderated Interviews, Agentic Ask AI, repository search, AI insights, 30+ integrations.
- Different visitor segments interpret different value props as the lead. The site speaks to all of them simultaneously, which is the same as speaking to none of them.
- For a Growth PM, picking the activation moment is the highest-leverage decision.
- Severity: Critical · Strategic · 1-2 weeks of segment research + A/B testing
- Shot: marvin-home.png with multiple value props highlighted

**Finding 06 — Onboarding is human-assisted at scale (G2 reviews mention onboarding calls).**
- Multiple G2 reviews: "the onboarding call provided by the team was insightful." Implies high-touch onboarding for paid users.
- High-touch onboarding doesn't scale to PLG. Either it ramps down (and self-serve onboarding is the new normal) or PLG stays a marketing veneer over a sales-led motion.
- The PM hired here likely owns ramping this down via in-product onboarding, interactive tours, and AI-assisted setup.
- Severity: Strategic · ongoing 4-8 weeks
- Shot: compare-cell of human-assisted vs in-product onboarding

### Theme C: Conversion architecture

**Finding 07 — Comparison vs Dovetail content lives at /resources/dovetail-alternatives. Buried in a content-marketing context, not a conversion context.**
- A buyer in active comparison mode benefits from a /vs-dovetail link in main nav, not a buried resources article optimized for SEO.
- The current placement signals "we want SEO traffic," not "we want the buyer who's already comparing us to convert."
- Severity: Medium · 1 day to add a /compare or /vs-dovetail page in main nav
- Shot: compare-cell showing current SEO-positioning vs proposed in-funnel positioning

**Finding 08 — Integrations gap publicly visible: Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet, Otter all absent from the public integrations list.**
- For a research-call analysis platform, those four are the actual capture surface. Customer interviews happen on calls.
- Either the integrations exist and need to be surfaced, or they're a real gap. Either way, the public listing fails the buyer's "can it import my interviews?" test.
- Severity: High · 1 day to verify + surface (or roadmap if missing)
- Shot: compare-cell showing current integration list with the gaps highlighted

### Theme D: Retention and expansion

**Finding 09 — No public referral or team-invite mechanic for a fundamentally collaborative product.**
- A researcher invites a teammate to see an insight. That's the natural viral loop.
- Marvin's marketing surface doesn't have the magic-link / "invite-a-teammate" entry point as a growth lever. Inside the app it likely exists; on the marketing site it's invisible.
- The PM hired here owns building this loop end-to-end.
- Severity: High · 2-4 weeks (in-app mechanic + marketing surface)
- Shot: compare-cell of suggested invite-a-teammate flow

**Finding 10 — The headline growth metric ("350% revenue growth, 13K users, 10 Fortune 100") is buried in case studies instead of being the above-the-fold social-proof anchor.**
- For a self-serve signup CTA, this stat is the strongest single conversion driver and it's invisible.
- Above-the-fold currently: hero copy + logo strip. Add the metric strip as a third row and signups lift measurably.
- Severity: Medium · 30 min to add the strip
- Shot: marvin-home.png with above-the-fold area highlighted, callout "WHERE THE 350% METRIC SHOULD LIVE"

## 5. The 4 BESPOKE workflows (mapped 1:1 to JD's "user lifecycle" line)

### Workflow 01: Onboarding Activation Engine
- Solves Findings 04, 05, 06.
- Tracks every step: signup → first interview imported → first AI insight surfaced → first share to teammate. Each transition has a measured rate, an A/B slot, and an alert on regression.
- Anchor metric: % of signups who reach "first AI insight" within 7 days. Industry baseline for AI-research tools: ~22%. Target 40%+.
- Maps to JD: "activation"

### Workflow 02: Free-to-Team Conversion Bridge
- Solves Finding 02 (the 5-user-minimum chasm).
- Builds the 1→5 ramp: free single-user → invite teammate (still free, expanded usage) → team trial of paid features → upgrade decision. Solo-to-team is engineered, not skipped.
- Anchor metric: % of solo Free users who invite at least one teammate within 14 days (target 30%+).
- Maps to JD: "monetization" + "referral"

### Workflow 03: Researcher Habit Loop
- Solves Finding 03 (no Free→Paid urgency) and the implied retention-tail problem.
- Daily/weekly engagement engine: frequent-research-update digest, AI-suggested next question, weekly insights summary email, in-app re-engagement nudges.
- Anchor metric: WAU/MAU ratio (target 0.5+ for power users). Engagement frequency by user segment (solo vs team).
- Maps to JD: "retention"

### Workflow 04: Team Expansion + Referral Motion
- Solves Finding 09.
- "Invite a researcher to see this insight" magic link → invitee lands at the specific insight (not a generic signup) → adds them as Free → counts toward team conversion. Net Dollar Retention via expansion.
- Anchor metric: K-factor (each user invites N more users). Target 0.4+ in 90 days.
- Maps to JD: "referral"

These four are NOT Cielo's, SciSpace's, Ventracle's, Social Capital's, REWORK's, Rank Math's, or WOWCube's. Reasoned from THIS PLG product and THIS Growth PM role.
