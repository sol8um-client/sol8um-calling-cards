System 01 · Onboarding Activation Engine · Day 0-7

Pick the activation moment.
Engineer everything around it.

Solves Findings 04, 05, 06. The site lists five value props simultaneously, which means new users hunt for the "aha" alone. This system picks the activation moment from data (the action that correlates highest with day-7 retention and day-30 paid conversion), instruments every step of the onboarding funnel, and runs in-product nudges that turn 22% week-1 activation into 40%+. The PM opens a Mixpanel cohort dashboard on Monday and sees the funnel, not a deck.

How it runs

STACK
Mixpanel / Amplitude (event tracking) + n8n (in-product nudges) + Customer.io / Iterable (lifecycle email) + Pendo / Userpilot (in-app tours).
REPLACES
The "we hope they figure it out" assumption. Human-assisted onboarding calls (Finding 06) for everyone except enterprise.
ANCHOR METRIC
% of signups who reach the picked activation moment within 7 days. Industry baseline for AI research tools: ~22%. Target 40%+.
