System 03 · Researcher Habit Loop · Day 3+ continuous

When does a researcher come back?
Engineer the answer.

Solves Finding 03 (no Free-to-Paid urgency) and the implied retention-tail problem. Researchers don't research every day. So Marvin's stickiness has to come from non-research-day triggers: the AI-suggested next question, the weekly insights summary, the "your teammate just imported an interview, here's what changed in your shared theme" notification. This system is the engagement engine. WAU/MAU ratio for power users is the anchor.

How it runs

STACK
Mixpanel WAU/MAU tracking + Customer.io lifecycle email + n8n notification orchestration + Claude-driven AI-suggested-next-question generation + in-product re-engagement nudges.
REPLACES
"User signed up, used it once, never came back" silent churn. The retention-tail problem that compounds quietly.
ANCHOR METRIC
WAU/MAU ratio for power users (target 0.5+). Weekly digest open rate (target 35%+). AI-suggested-question CTR (target 18%+).
The 4 re-engagement triggers
TRIGGER 01 · WEEKLY DIGEST
Friday 09:00 local: "Your week in research"
5 themes that surfaced this week, 1 cross-interview pattern Claude found, 1 question worth asking next. Email + in-app card.
TRIGGER 02 · AI-SUGGESTED QUESTION
In-product, on next login
"Try asking: 'What did users say when they got stuck on onboarding?'" Auto-generated from their corpus, not generic.
TRIGGER 03 · TEAMMATE ACTIVITY
"Aisha imported 3 interviews. Here's what changed in your shared theme."
Notification when teammate activity affects shared insights. Pulls them back not for FOMO, but for substance.
TRIGGER 04 · DORMANT BACK-NUDGE
Day 14 of inactivity: "We saved your work"
Soft, non-pushy. "Your repository has 12 themes. Here's the one that aged well." Single-CTA back to the product.
EACH TRIGGER A/B TESTABLE · OFF-DAY ENGAGEMENT, NOT NOTIFICATION-SPAM
