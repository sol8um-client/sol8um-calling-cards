System 02 · Free-to-Team Conversion Bridge · Day 5-25

The 5-user-minimum chasm,
bridged.

Solves Finding 02. Free is solo-only. Standard requires 5 users minimum. The 1-to-5 ramp doesn't exist. A solo Free user who's hooked has nowhere to go. This system builds the missing bridge: solo activated user invites teammate (still free), teammate invites another, when 5 reached the team auto-trials Standard. The product engineers team formation. The PM watches the conversion arc, not the spreadsheet.

How it runs

STACK
Shopify-style team-formation logic in product (custom build) + Customer.io lifecycle email + Mixpanel cohort tracking + n8n for trigger orchestration.
REPLACES
The "solo Free user wants to upgrade but can't get 4 colleagues to commit" cliff. Solo-to-team conversion that depends on luck.
ANCHOR METRIC
% of solo Free users who invite at least one teammate within 14 days. Target 30%+. Then % of those teams that reach 5 users (Standard auto-trial). Target 40%+.
