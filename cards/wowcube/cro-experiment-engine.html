System 01 · CRO Experiment Engine · continuous

Every experiment instrumented.
Every learning logged.

Most CRO programs lose half their value because experiments ship without a hypothesis log, and the next experiment forgets what the last one taught. This system runs the full experiment lifecycle on Convert.com, Shopify, and Smartlook as one connected operating model. Hypothesis from session-recording analysis, variant build, split-test config, significance gating, ship-or-kill decision, learnings log. The PM opens the engine on Monday and sees what's running, what reached significance, what shipped last week.

How it runs

STACK
Convert.com (split testing) + Shopify (variant control) + Smartlook (session recording) + Notion (hypothesis log) + Slack (weekly digest). Glued by n8n where Convert.com's native automations don't reach.
REPLACES
Experiments shipped without a hypothesis log. The "we tested that 6 months ago, what happened?" amnesia. Tests killed prematurely or left running too long because nobody owns the significance gate.
ANCHOR METRIC
Experiments shipped per quarter (target 10-15) with a 25%+ win rate. Every experiment, win or lose, has a learnings log entry compounding across quarters.
