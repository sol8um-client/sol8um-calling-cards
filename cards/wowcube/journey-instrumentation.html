Sample · Step 03 → Step 04 hop diagnostic
Subdomain leak
@cro — Hop Diagnostic · wowcube.com -> shop.wowcube.com
CART COOKIE · LOST on 38% of hop transitions
Root cause: Shopify cart cookie scoped to shop.wowcube.com only. Pre-hop interest doesn't carry over.
Fix: explicit cross-subdomain cookie write on hop OR consolidate to single domain.
GA SESSION · SPLIT on ~92% of hops (no linker configured)
Effect: GA reports two separate sessions per buyer. Conversion attribution to original source is broken.
Fix: GA4 cross-domain config with linker on both subdomains. Standard 30-min config in GA admin.
CONVERT.COM SCOPE · PER-DOMAIN
Effect: A/B tests can't span the hop. A test on the marketing site can't measure post-hop conversion impact.
Fix: Convert.com cross-domain mode (paid plan feature) or run paired tests with shared event signal.
SMARTLOOK SESSION · BREAKS mid-funnel
Effect: session recordings show pre-hop OR post-hop, never both. Half the funnel observation is missing.
Fix: Smartlook cross-domain tracking (built-in feature, not enabled by default).
Repair plan: 1-day config week 1. Funnel reporting becomes accurate same week.
