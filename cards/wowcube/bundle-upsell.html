System 04 · Bundle and Upsell Intelligence · week 1 ship + ongoing A/B

AOV math, not vibe math.
Bundle merchandising done right.

Solves Finding 08 directly. The current PDP shows variants in a dropdown: Standalone $499, Bundle $439, Ultimate $499. Same price as standalone, drastically more value. A buyer who notices feels rewarded; a buyer who doesn't picks the worse option without realizing. This system replaces the dropdown with a visual comparison block, adds cart-time upsells, and runs a post-purchase cross-sell sequence. AOV lift target: +15-25% in 60 days.

@cro — Bundle and Upsell Funnel · AOV ladder
PDP · PURCHASE EVENT Buyer picks Ultimate Bundle ($499). AOV at PDP: $499
"Most picked" social-proof badge nudges 28% of buyers from standalone to Ultimate. Net AOV lift on this conversion alone: +$0 (same price) but more product shipped, lower future-accessory-revenue dependency.
CART · UPSELL TRIGGER Standalone-only buyer sees: "Add an Extra Dock for charging on the road - $29"
Industry attach rate for cart-time accessory upsells: 18-25%. Adds $5-7 AOV per cart.
POST-PURCHASE · DAY 7 Klaviyo email: "Your cube has 4 game packs. Here are 6 more your kid (or you) will love."
Game-pack cross-sell. Industry post-purchase email-driven AOV lift: 8-15% on lifetime customer value.
POST-PURCHASE · DAY 30 "Your firmware just shipped v3.3. Here's what's new + 2 new games tested by your community."
Doubles as proof-of-quality (ties to System 03) and a soft-sell for the next game pack. Engagement loop, not a hard ask.
POST-PURCHASE · DAY 90 "It's been 3 months. How is your cube? Leave a review for $10 off your next order."
Captures verified reviews for System 03's reviews block. Closes the loop between PDP trust and customer experience.
Net AOV target: +$60-75 per order in 60 days · LTV target: +20% on 12-month cohort
