The job post wants 1-2 demos.
Build the engine that makes one per prospect.

A founding engineer who hand-builds two demos has built two demos. One who builds the Demo Engine has built a system that turns any prospect into a working, personalized demo within a day. That is what lets the closer walk into every call with something real on screen, wired to data that looks like the prospect's own business, instead of a slide that says "imagine if."
