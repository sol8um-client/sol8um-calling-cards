System 02 · Scope to Signed · close the deal

A discovery call shouldn't end in a blank page.
It should end in a proposal.

The gap between "great call" and a signed client is a proposal, and writing one from a blank page every time is where warm deals quietly go cold. Scope to Signed turns the call into a scoped, priced, productized proposal in minutes, with every process the prospect mentioned mapped onto the three-tier offering. The closer reviews it and sends the same day, while the prospect still remembers the conversation.

How it runs

STACK
Claude reads the call transcript or notes, extracts the automatable processes, and maps each onto the productized menu from the blueprint. n8n drafts the proposal document.
REPLACES
The proposal written from scratch after every call. The inconsistency of quoting each deal a different way. Deals lost in the days-long gap after a good conversation.
ANCHOR METRIC
Proposal turnaround, from days to same-day. And scoping consistency: every deal measured against the same menu, so pricing stops being a guess.
