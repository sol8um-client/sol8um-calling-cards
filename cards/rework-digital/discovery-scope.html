System 01 · Discovery to Scope Engine · Day 0 of every engagement

A 30-min discovery call goes in.
A milestone-broken SOW comes out.

The single biggest reason marketplaces lose to in-house hires is scoping speed. A business books a discovery call, the agency takes a week to come back with an estimate, and by then the prospect has either lost momentum or hired someone they already trust. This system runs the scoping pass in 90 minutes. Discovery call → transcript → process extraction → ROI ranking → milestone-broken scope of work, ready for client review same day.
