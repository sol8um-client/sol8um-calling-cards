## 4. The 10 audit findings (4-theme spine bespoke to a 2025 marketplace selling "proof of work")

### Theme A: The proof-of-work paradox (the loudest gap)

**Finding 01 — Brand promise is "proof of work verified" but zero proof of work is visible without signup**
- Hero verbatim: "One platform to hire, build, prove, and grow"
- The verb "prove" is the brand wedge, but a visitor sees zero actual proven work before being asked to sign up
- For a marketplace selling trust, the trust-source must be visible BEFORE the trust-ask
- Severity: Critical · 1 day fix (surface 3-5 actual milestone-verified deliverables on the public site)

**Finding 02 — 4 case studies on the homepage, 0 named clients**
- "SaaS support: 68% auto-resolution, $480K savings" — anonymized
- "D2C ecommerce: 3.0x conversion" — anonymized
- "FinTech: <1s latency, $240K savings" — anonymized
- "Logistics AP: -85% time, 99.2% accuracy" — anonymized
- A marketplace selling trust, with anonymized case studies, is a brand contradiction. Buyers can't verify the verification platform's verification.
- Severity: Critical · 4 hr (get permission from one client, name them; a single named case unlocks the rest)

**Finding 03 — Trust-tier methodology (Bronze → Diamond) isn't documented publicly**
- Trust tiers are the core trust mechanism
- The methodology (what gets you from Bronze to Diamond) isn't on the site
- Both audiences need to see this: businesses to understand what tier means, experts to know what the path looks like
- Severity: High · 30 min (one page describing the criteria)

### Theme B: Dual-audience failure (marketplace serves both, site optimizes for neither)

**Finding 04 — No expert portfolios browsable from the public site**
- Businesses arriving to evaluate cannot preview what they'd get
- "Browse Projects" link in footer goes to a gated page
- Toptal lets you browse case-anonymized expert profiles publicly. REWORK could too.
- Severity: High · 2 hr (build a public read-only tier of the expert directory)

**Finding 05 — No expert testimonials surfaced, anywhere**
- Applying experts (like Nihal right now reading this site) cannot tell what success on the platform looks like
- "I made $X in my first quarter on REWORK" type testimonials are the standard pull for two-sided marketplaces
- Severity: Medium · 4 hr (interview 3 existing experts, publish quotes)

**Finding 06 — "How it works" page is gated behind signup**
- The literal explanation of the marketplace mechanics requires conversion before education
- Inverts the standard funnel
- Severity: High · 1 hr (move "How it works" content to a public page; gate the actual sign-up flow not the explanation)

### Theme C: Thin social proof for a 2025 startup

**Finding 07 — 47 LinkedIn followers for a marketplace promising network value**
- Network-effect businesses live or die on visible network signals
- 47 followers tells the visitor "this is too early" before they read a word
- Severity: High · ongoing (founder-led content + employee post amplification + cross-link from site)

**Finding 08 — Founder voice not surfaced on the site**
- Anslem Ebiega doesn't appear on the homepage by name or photo
- Founder-led marketplaces (Replit, Lovable, Toptal) all surface the founder
- Severity: High · 30 min ("Built by Anslem Ebiega" block on the homepage)

**Finding 09 — No newsletter, no email capture, no follow-the-founder CTA**
- Same capture-infrastructure gap that haunts most early-stage sites
- For a 2025 startup, every visitor who leaves without leaving a contact is gone
- Severity: Compounding · 30 min

### Theme D: Brand sprawl

**Finding 10 — Footer lists 5 sibling products that dilute the marketplace pitch**
- Finprob, SwitchAsses, Loevech, RECI, Resume Builder all appear in the main footer
- For a 2025 marketplace still building category awareness, these are noise
- Move them to subdomains or a separate /products page; keep the main nav focused on the marketplace promise
- Severity: Strategic · 1 hr

## 5. The 4 BESPOKE workflows (each maps to REWORK's actual case studies)

### Workflow 01: Discovery → Scope Engine
- **What it is:** intake automation for incoming REWORK client requests. Takes a 30-min discovery call recording, extracts business processes (Whisper transcript → Claude extraction), identifies high-ROI automation candidates, ranks by effort/return, generates a milestone-broken scope-of-work draft
- **Anchor metric:** time from "discovery call recorded" to "milestone-broken SOW ready" — target <90 min, was 1-2 weeks
- **Replaces:** manual intake → 2-week scoping → estimate. Kills the speed-to-quote bottleneck.
- **Why this matters for REWORK:** their model lives or dies on quote turnaround. Faster scoping = more closed engagements = more revenue per expert
- **Stack:** Whisper / AssemblyAI → n8n + Claude → Notion / SOW PDF generator

### Workflow 02: AI Support Agent + Eval Loop
- **What it is:** Claude/OpenAI-powered support bot with intelligent escalation to humans, handoff-resolution learning loop that expands tier-1 coverage monthly, eval framework that catches quality regressions
- **Anchor metric:** auto-resolution rate (target 60-70%, matching their published case study) maintained month-over-month without quality drift
- **Replaces:** building a support bot from scratch each time + the "we shipped a bot but it got worse over time" silent failure
- **Why this matters for REWORK:** their SaaS support case study (68% auto-resolution, $480K savings) is THE flagship outcome. This workflow shows I can replicate it.
- **Stack:** Intercom / Front API → n8n + Claude classifier + handoff trigger → eval suite (golden set, regression test) → CI alert on drift

### Workflow 03: Lead Pipeline Builder Kit
- **What it is:** modular n8n workflows (lead scoring + Apollo enrichment + CRM sync + multi-channel sequence + sales handoff) configurable per client in under 2 days
- **Anchor metric:** time to deliver a full working lead pipeline per client (target <2 days, was ~2 weeks)
- **Replaces:** building each pipeline custom from scratch. Modular components reused across clients.
- **Why this matters for REWORK:** their D2C case study (3.0x conversion, +220% email rev) is from this kind of system. Reusable modules = faster delivery = higher gross margin per engagement.
- **Stack:** Apollo / Clearbit + HubSpot / Salesforce + n8n + Smartlead / Instantly + Slack

### Workflow 04: Reliability & Proof-of-Work Layer
- **What it is:** auto-generates client status updates from automation logs, flags reliability issues before clients notice, packages milestone evidence in the format REWORK's marketplace verifies
- **Anchor metric:** time from "issue starts" to "client visibility" — target same-day, was 1-3 weeks
- **Replaces:** "is the automation still working?" anxiety. Both client and operator see the same log surface.
- **Why this matters for REWORK:** this is literally on-brand. REWORK sells "proof of work verified deliverables." This workflow IS the proof-of-work layer applied to ongoing automation reliability, not just initial milestone delivery. The card itself demonstrates this concept.
- **Stack:** automation logs (Postgres / Supabase) → n8n monitor → Slack alerts + auto-status PDF + REWORK milestone evidence packager
