System 03 · Lead Pipeline Builder Kit · D2C ecommerce case study

Modular pipelines, configured per client.
Each one strengthens the next.

The biggest hidden cost on a contractor marketplace is rebuilding the same pipeline from scratch for each new client. A 2-week build for client A is then a 2-week build for client B, then for C. This system replaces that pattern with a kit of reusable modules (lead scoring, Apollo enrichment, CRM sync, multi-channel sequencing, sales handoff). Each engagement reuses 60-70% of prior modules. Configuration time drops to under 2 days per client. Maps to your published 3.0x conversion / +220% email revenue case study.
