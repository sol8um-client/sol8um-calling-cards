Where reworkdigital.io is leaving
the proof-of-work promise unproven.

Your brand promise is "proof of work verified marketplace." That's a strong category claim. The audit below is built around one question: does the site visibly prove that promise to either of your two audiences (the business hiring an expert, the expert applying to join) before it asks them to sign up? Each finding pairs the actual page evidence with what it costs the visitor and how fast it's fixed.

Audited live · 2026-05-05 · reworkdigital.io · founded 2025 · 47 LinkedIn followers · 4 anonymized case studies
Reading the room

Two audiences arrive at reworkdigital.io. Site optimizes for neither.

A two-sided marketplace has the dual-audience problem in its DNA. Businesses hiring need to trust the experts. Experts joining need to trust the platform. The same homepage has to do both jobs. Right now, three buyer types arrive and bounce off equally hard.

Strategic
Buyer 01 · Business hiring
Operations lead, $50K-$200K to spend
SaaS / D2C / FinTech / Logistics. Found you via a search or referral. Wants proof you've done work like theirs at the quality and reliability your "proof of work verified" tagline promises. Decision: post a job and pick an expert.
Fit signal 65%
Buyer 02 · Expert applying
AI / automation builder
Reading your JD or finding you via Wellfound. Wants to see what success on the platform looks like, what other experts have made, what the verification path is, what payments look like. Decision: apply, or move to Toptal / Contra.
Fit signal 55%
Buyer 03 · Strategic partner
Investor / press / acquirer
Researching the AI marketplace category. Wants to see growth signals, named clients, founder voice, product depth. Currently sees 47 LinkedIn followers, anonymized case studies, no founder presence on the homepage. Misses the actual story.
Fit signal 35%
Three things already working
"Proof of work verified marketplace" is genuinely differentiated. Toptal, Upwork, Contra all sell experts; only REWORK frames the verification layer as the wedge. Trust-tier system + milestone-based escrow is a smart trust mechanism that solves the cold-start problem without requiring social proof first. Toronto-Eastern-Time hiring with full-async culture matches the product model: deliverables-over-meetings. None of those need touching. The findings below are about how the site fails to make them visible.

Three findings. Your brand wedge is "proof of work verified." The site doesn't show the work.
01

Brand promise is "proof of work verified" but zero proof of work is visible without signup, to either side.

Critical · 1 day
EVIDENCE
Hero verbatim: "One platform to hire, build, prove, and grow." The verb "prove" is the brand wedge. A visitor sees zero actual proven work before being asked to sign up. "Proof of Work" exists as a footer link, but the page behind it is gated. For a marketplace selling trust, the trust-source has to be visible BEFORE the trust-ask.
